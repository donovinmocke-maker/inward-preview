Work with Katie

Life Coaching

You probably don’t need someone else telling you what to do. You need space to hear yourself clearly enough to know. Coaching with me is about creating that space.

The approach

Not a blueprint. Your own voice.

I’m not here to hand you a blueprint for your life. I’m here to help you become more honest with yourself, trust your own voice, and make decisions from a place that feels grounded and true.

You’re not looking to blow up your life. You’re looking to feel alive inside it again.

How it begins

Suggested flow — Katie to confirm

ii.

A first conversation

iii.

The inward work

Sessions shaped around you, with space between them to notice, feel, and practise.
